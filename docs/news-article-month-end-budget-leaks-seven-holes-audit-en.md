            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Pitfalls and One-Line Patches</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Patch scenes first, not rerolls.</strong> The audio default is one checkbox per template and returns 1,440 without changing a single creative decision — highest return-per-minute hole in the set. Reroll conversion (4,704) is bigger but slower; sequence it second.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Budget in list, pay in meters.</strong> Wallets and grants are denominated in credits; capacity in list. Convert once, at the plan rate (82/73/63), and every "overrun" becomes a subtraction problem with a known answer.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Re-check the ladder when volume changes.</strong> The 990 / 2,290 / 3,417 breakevens are monthly; a quarter that drifts from 800 to 1,500 list should move you from wallet to Basic without a meeting.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Count bytes for CJK scripts, per lane.</strong> One extra column in the audio budget (UTF-8 bytes, not characters) closes hole 4 permanently; the turbo-vs-multilingual 2× is hole 4's quieter twin.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Convert flat operations to per-second before adopting them.</strong> An extend is only cheap until you divide it. Four 360-credit continuations a month is a per-second model waiting to happen.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Fund the canary line.</strong> 186 credits a month of 6-credit rehearsals is the hole whose patch is a purchase — and against the 882-credit rework gap it returned in the post-mortem, it is the cheapest insurance on the sheet.</p>
