            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Pipeline Failure Modes</h2>
            <ul style="color:#d1d5db;">
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Contract mismatches between stations.</strong> Flare only offers transparent backgrounds at 1K, but decomposition wants a definite size (no auto) within its pixel window — a 4K scene must be down-planned before Station 2, not after a rejection.</li>
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Aspect ratio is decided at Station 1, forever.</strong> Turbo's image-to-video keeps the source frame; a square hero image begets a square clip no matter what the marketplace prefers later.</li>
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Drafting in the wrong currency.</strong> The per-second stations make iteration expensive — that's why Wan 3.0's 480p tier (12/sec) exists in this pipeline even though it ships nothing final.</li>
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Counting generations, not accepts.</strong> This invoice assumes one take per station. Real acceptance rates are 2–3× on faces and reflective surfaces; matte metal on windowsills was chosen for the demo partly because it's forgiving.</li>
