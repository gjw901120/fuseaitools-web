            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Four Failure Modes of Agency Credit Structure</h2>
            <ul style="color:#d1d5db;">
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">One pool for everyone is cheap only until the first dispute.</strong> Wallet 2's $2.72 advantage evaporates the month a client questions 2,000 credits of shared drain you can't attribute; structure is insurance, and insurance is priced correctly when the claim would cost more.</li>
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Seats kept alive past their burst.</strong> A weekly Ultra renewed for a project that went quiet pays a fee against zero consumption — seats open with a work order and close when the week does. Four zombie seats cost more per month than the entire overhead line.</li>
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Grant spill on oversized weeks.</strong> The flat-$7.90 week works under ~1,510 list credits; a 2,400-credit sprint week tops up inside the seat at 68% when the shared monthly pool would have metered it at 63%. Know which side of the crossover a client's week falls on before opening the seat.</li>
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Quoting media at metered cost.</strong> Invoice against list, keep the plan spread — a client who learns your media actually cost $12 of a $450 package has a repricing argument for renewal month, and so does every competitor who sees your line items.</li>
