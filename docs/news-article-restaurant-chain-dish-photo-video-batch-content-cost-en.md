            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Pitfalls: Five Ways a Chain Kit Still Balloons</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Rendering every dish at 4K.</strong> Resolution is money on this lane: 40 heroes at 4K is 480 instead of the 360 a 2K render costs, and the extra pixels are deleted the moment the plate shows in a 240px app thumbnail. Pick resolution per deliverable, not per "quality" feeling.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Asking for a transparent PNG at 2K.</strong> The background flag on GPT 2.5 Flare only exists at 1K; request alpha higher up and the order can bill the 18-credit tier and hand back an opaque file. Transparency decides the working resolution — plan the composite pass around it.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Re-generating a hero plate to fix one garnish.</strong> A fresh roll re-rolls the whole approved dish. A masked edit is 6 credits and changes only the flagged region; on a forty-dish menu the reshoot reflex quietly doubles your stills line.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Shipping store videos at 1080p for every door.</strong> Per-second billing means a 10-second hero costs 360 on Turbo 1080p; the ambient store screen rarely needs it. Cut the volume of seconds first — shorter loops on the 720p lane at 27/s is the biggest single lever on the sheet.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Long, uncut promo scripts.</strong> Voice-over bills UTF-8 bytes, so a rambling announcement pays more blocks for the same message. Rehearse the read down to ~1,800 bytes; the VO line is meant to be a rounding error, not a line item.</p>
