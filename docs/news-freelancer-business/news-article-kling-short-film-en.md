            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Musicians need music videos.             <div style="background:rgba(31,41,55,0.3);padding:16px;border-radius:8px;margin:16px 0;">
                <p style="color:#f87171;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Music video production workflow:</p>
                <p style="color:#f87171;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">1) Listen to the track. Identify mood shifts, emotional beats, tempo changes</p>
                <p style="color:#f87171;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">2) Develop visual concept: narrative, abstract, performance, or hybrid approach</p>
                <p style="color:#f87171;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">3) Create shot list synced to song structure (verse, chorus, bridge, outro)</p>
                <p style="color:#f87171;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">4) Generate video segments for each section (30–60 second clips)</p>
                <p style="color:#f87171;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">5) Edit segments into complete video with music sync</p>
                <p style="color:#f87171;font-style:italic;">6) Color grade, add transitions, final polish</p>
