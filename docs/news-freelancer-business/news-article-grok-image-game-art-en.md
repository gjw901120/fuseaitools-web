            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Indie studios need concept art for pitch decks, Kickstarter campaigns, and early development direction. You deliver 20–30 concept pieces exploring the game's visual identity — enough to establish art direction and impress potential publishers or backers.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Game Pitch Concept Package</h3>
            <div style="background:rgba(31,41,55,0.3);padding:16px;border-radius:8px;margin:16px 0;">
                <p style="color:#94a3b8;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Package deliverables:</p>
                <p style="color:#94a3b8;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">1) Art direction board: 10 style exploration images defining the visual tone</p>
                <p style="color:#94a3b8;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">2) Character concepts: 5 key character designs with 3 variations each</p>
                <p style="color:#94a3b8;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">3) Environment concepts: 5 key locations with mood and lighting studies</p>
                <p style="color:#94a3b8;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">4) Key art: 3 hero images suitable for Steam page, pitch deck cover, or marketing</p>
                <p style="color:#94a3b8;font-style:italic;">5) Style guide document: prompt templates and visual rules for maintaining consistency in production</p>
            </div>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Pricing:</strong> $2,000–3,000 for basic concept package (20 pieces). $4,000–6,000 for premium with key art, marketing visuals, and detailed style guide.</p>

            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Service Line 2: Character Design Sets ($3,000–8,000)</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Games need characters — protagonists, NPCs, enemies, bosses. You deliver complete character design packages: front/side/back views, expression sheets, color variations, and costume alternatives. Each character is production-ready for a 3D modeler or 2D animator to work from.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: 10-Character RPG Cast</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Design a complete RPG cast: 3 protagonists (warrior, mage, rogue archetypes), 4 NPCs (quest givers, merchants, allies), 3 enemies (grunt, elite, boss). Each character gets: full-body front view, 3/4 view, detail close-ups of key design elements, 3 color palette variations, and an expression sheet (neutral, angry, happy, determined). Delivered as organized asset folders with design notes for the development team.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Pricing:</strong> $300–500 per character for standard designs. $500–800 for complex characters with multiple costumes and detailed turnaround sheets.</p>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Game worlds need environmental storytelling.
