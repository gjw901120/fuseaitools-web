                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Reference hoarding.</strong> Mini's with_video scene makes references <em>cheaper</em> (3/sec at 480p) — channels that still render without_video at 6/sec are paying double for worse motion fidelity.</li>
