            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">II. Aleph 2.0 & Edit Studio: Entering the Era of "Pixel-Level Control"</h2>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">In May 2026, Runway released <strong>Aleph 2.0</strong>—a comprehensive upgrade to its flagship video editing model—alongside the companion product <strong>Edit Studio</strong>.</p>

            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Scalpel Editing: Change Only What You Want</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Aleph 2.0's core breakthrough is <strong>precise input video preservation</strong>. The problem with most AI video editing models: you ask it to change one thing, and it reimagines the entire video. New shots are added, objects are modified, scene actions are altered—you no longer have the original video.</p>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Aleph 2.0's solution: <strong>targeted editing</strong>—only change what you ask for; everything else stays exactly as is. When the rest of the video must match the original, this fidelity determines the difference between a "usable edit" and a "derailed mess."</p>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Experience Aleph 2.0 editing: <a href="https://www.fuseaitools.com/home/runway/aleph" style="color:#60a5fa;">Runway Aleph</a>.</p>

            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Image-Level Control: Preview Before You Commit</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Aleph 2.0 also introduces <strong>"image-level video editing control":</strong> give the model one frame containing the edit you want, and it propagates that edit across the entire video.</p>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Other AI video models work by: you describe the edit, the model interprets it, and you wait until full generation completes to see the result. Aleph 2.0 works differently—you pre-define what the edit should look like with a single frame, and the model applies it precisely to the correct position in the video. This dramatically reduces the iteration cycle of "pull the slot machine lever."</p>

            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">III. Media Router: Runway's "Masterstroke"—Don't Build the One Model, Route All Models</h2>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">On July 23, 2026, Runway released a tool with even greater strategic significance—<strong>Runway Media Router</strong>.</p>

            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">What Is It?</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Media Router is an <strong>AI model router</strong>. When generating images, video, or audio, it automatically selects the most suitable AI model based on three criteria: <strong>quality, processing speed, and cost</strong>.</p>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">The system can automatically switch models based on the priority of each request, reducing the burden on developers to compare and manually switch models. For example:</p>
            <ul style="color:#d1d5db;">
                <li><strong>Creative validation phase</strong> → prioritize speed</li>
                <li><strong>Final output phase</strong> → prioritize quality</li>
            </ul>

            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Why It Matters</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Runway Media Router is currently live on the Runway Dev developer platform, supporting image, video, and audio generation. The models it can route to include not only Runway's own Gen-4, but also <strong>Seedance, GPT Image 2, ElevenLabs</strong>, and others.</p>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">The implication is profound: <strong>Runway is no longer trying to win with a single model.</strong> It is actively acknowledging that "no single model is universal" and instead providing a model orchestration system.</p>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Runway co-founder and co-CEO Anastasis Germanidis put it bluntly: "The more AI-generated scenes an ad campaign or multi-scene video project has, the more no single model can handle it all—the infrastructure for multi-model mixing becomes essential."</p>

            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">The Strategic Arc: Runway's "Platform" Ambition</h3>
