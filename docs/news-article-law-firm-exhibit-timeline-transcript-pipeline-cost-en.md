            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Pitfalls: Five Ways a Trial-Prep Budget Breaks</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Transcribing full recordings instead of quoted passages.</strong> Per-minute billing with a ceiling makes raw hour-long depositions the fastest line on the sheet. Excerpt to the passages the exhibits cite — this month's same six witnesses cost 270/file excerpted and would have cleared 3,000 raw.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Re-rendering exhibits after review.</strong> A regenerated timeline re-rolls verified content; changed pixels between review passes are an impeachment argument before they are a cost. Corrections are flat-6 masked edits; keep the parent file and the audit trail.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Paying the 2K cliff on drafts.</strong> On the Pro lane 2K doubles the unit to 24; iterating chronology bands there is paying finals prices for throwaways. The flat-12 standard lane bills 12 at any allowed resolution — draft there, render finals on Pro.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Screenshots of originals as exhibits.</strong> Discovery PDFs carry privileged text, metadata, and identifying data. Rebuild as synthetic diagrams (decomposition at 12/24, illustrations at 9) and the compliance argument is settled by construction, not by redaction passes.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">30-second "reconstructions" for openings.</strong> Duration is billed per second (27/s at 720p) and argument risk scales with it the same way. Five seconds illustrates; thirty seconds argues. Judges object to the second one and invoices do too.</p>
