            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Pitfalls: Five Ways a Small Shop Balloons a Small Month</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Requesting transparent backgrounds at 2K.</strong> The alpha flag is a 1K capability; higher requests can bill 18 and still deliver an opaque file. If the pipeline needs compositing, the working resolution is 1K — plan every cutout there and upsell the scene, not the sprite.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">"Regenerating the same candle in blue."</strong> A fresh generation of a variant is a new object: the jar drifts, the label re-wraps, the review photo stops matching. The edit lane is flat 6 and shape-preserving; the re-roll is six credits and a refund dispute.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Treating each season as a new shoot.</strong> Holiday thumbnails generated from scratch are slower and pricier than re-scening evergreen cutouts. Twelve seasonal covers at flat-6 lite is a 72-credit decision; a "reshoot everything" habit is a lost December.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Badge text at thumbnail prices.</strong> Gift-guide covers carry real typographic load; the flat lanes will misspell at scale. Badges belong at 24 on the dense-text lane — 13% of the month, and the only line where a five-credit save costs a screenshot.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Shipping reels without drafts.</strong> The 48-credit draft is the insurance policy: a bad process angle discovered on the mini lane costs 48, the same discovery on the Turbo final costs 270. One draft per concept is not optional overhead.</p>
