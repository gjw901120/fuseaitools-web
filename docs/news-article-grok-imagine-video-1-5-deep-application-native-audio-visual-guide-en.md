            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Grok Imagine Video 1.5's audio capability is its core differentiator. Voices are clearer, lip-sync is more precise, and dialogue can be specified directly in the prompt.</p>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Dialogue writing rules:</p>
            <ul style="color:#d1d5db;">
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;">Keep lines short — one sentence per shot is more accurate</li>
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;">Specify tone and delivery (calm, confident, upbeat)</li>
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;">Explicitly state background music volume or "no background music"</li>
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;">Separate sound descriptions from visual descriptions on their own line</li>
            </ul>
