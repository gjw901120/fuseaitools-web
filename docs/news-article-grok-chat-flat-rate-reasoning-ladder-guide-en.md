            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workflow 1 — The Version-Cycling Regression Harness</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">When every model on a ladder costs the same, running your eval suite on all three stops being a luxury. Trend-analysis and social-monitoring teams can send the identical brief to grok-4-5, 4-6, and 4-7 at the same budget and diff the answers — measuring what a generation actually improved, in their domain, on their data.</p>

            <div style="background:rgba(31,41,55,0.3);padding:16px;border-radius:8px;margin:16px 0;">

                <p style="color:#9ca3af;">Pin the thinking level when comparing versions, or you're measuring the dial, not the model.</p>
            </div>

            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workflow 2 — Split the Pipeline: Search Off the Thinking Path</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">The one constraint that reorganizes everything: these Grok models disable tools and web search while extended thinking is enabled. The sound architecture is therefore two-stage rather than one mega-call:</p>

            <ol style="color:#d1d5db;">
                <li style="margin-bottom:8px;"><strong style="color:#60a5fa;">Stage 1 — Gather at low/no thinking.</strong> Fresh-signal collection (current events, live sentiment pulls) runs with thinking dialled down so search stays available, keeping retrieval cheap at 249 credits/M input.</li>
                <li style="margin-bottom:8px;"><strong style="color:#60a5fa;">Stage 2 — Reason over the snapshot at high/xhigh.</strong> Paste the collected material into a thinking-enabled call and let the model do adversarial reading — causal chains, contrarian takes, scenario trees — with no retrieval needed because the evidence is already in the prompt.</li>
            </ol>

            <div style="background:rgba(31,41,55,0.3);padding:16px;border-radius:8px;margin:16px 0;">
                <p style="color:#60a5fa;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Sizing note: reasoning output is billed output. A high-thinking analytical pass generating 2,000 tokens costs ~1.5 credits at 741/M — trivial alone, but multiplied across a 200-request daily queue it's 300 credits/day list, so set the level per job class instead of globally maxing xhigh.</p>
                <p style="color:#9ca3af;">The model's reasoning trace is not returned as visible text on these Grok endpoints — you get the answer, not the scratchpad. Bake that into expectations: verification comes from cross-version checks (Workflow 1), not chain-of-thought inspection. Where visible reasoning output matters, the <a href="https://www.fuseaitools.com/home/kimi" style="color:#60a5fa;">Kimi</a> endpoint exposes reasoning summaries instead.</p>
