            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Create virtual exhibitions that visitors can explore from anywhere in the world. AI generates immersive 3D exhibition spaces — gallery rooms with proper lighting, spatial flow, and curatorial context — populated with collection items. Visitors navigate the space as they would a physical exhibition, reading labels, examining artifacts, and experiencing the curatorial narrative.</p>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Many cultural artifacts are damaged, incomplete, or exist only in historical records. AI reconstructs damaged artifacts to their original appearance, visualizes archaeological finds in their original context, and creates 3D models of items too fragile for physical display.</p>
