        <section class="historical-reconstruction">
            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 1: Historical Scene Reconstruction</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Documentaries about historical events inevitably face moments where no footage exists. AI reconstructs historical scenes with period-accurate detail — environments, clothing, architecture, and atmosphere — based on historical research and reference materials. Clearly labeled as reconstruction, these scenes provide visual context that static photos and illustrations cannot.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Historical Reconstruction</h3>
            <div style="background:rgba(31,41,55,0.3);padding:16px;border-radius:8px;margin:16px 0;">
                <p style="color:#fb923c;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Reconstruction workflow:</p>
                <p style="color:#fb923c;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">1) Historical research: gather reference materials — paintings, photographs, written descriptions, archaeological evidence</p>
                <p style="color:#fb923c;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">2) Scene definition: describe the moment to reconstruct — location, time, people, actions, atmosphere</p>
                <p style="color:#fb923c;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">3) AI generation: "Documentary-style reconstruction. [Year/period]. [Location]. [People described]. [Action occurring]. Period-accurate [clothing/architecture/vehicles]. Natural lighting consistent with [time of day/weather]. Cinematic but documentary tone. No anachronistic elements."</p>
                <p style="color:#fb923c;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">4) Historical review: subject matter expert verifies accuracy</p>
                <p style="color:#fb923c;font-style:italic;">5) Label clearly: "AI-generated reconstruction based on historical research" — always visible on screen</p>
            </div>
        </section>
