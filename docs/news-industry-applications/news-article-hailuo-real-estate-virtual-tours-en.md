            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 2: Virtual Staging and Renovation Visualization</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Empty rooms look cold in photos. Staged homes sell 73% faster. But physical staging costs $2,000–10,000 per property. AI generates virtually staged walkthrough videos — showing empty rooms furnished with modern, appealing interiors. Similarly, renovation visualization shows buyers what a dated property could look like after remodeling.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Virtual Staging Video</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Take an empty apartment and generate two walkthrough videos: one showing the empty space (for buyers who want to design their own), and one showing it fully staged with contemporary furniture, art, and decor. Same camera path, same rooms — but the staged version helps buyers visualize living there. For renovation properties, generate before-and-after videos showing current state and remodeled potential.</p>
