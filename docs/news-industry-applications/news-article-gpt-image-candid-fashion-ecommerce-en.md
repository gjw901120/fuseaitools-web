            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 1: Candid Product Lifestyle Generation</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Replace sterile white-background product shots with lifestyle imagery that shows garments in real-world contexts. The candid approach means images look like they were captured by a friend's phone camera — natural lighting, real streets, authentic poses. This is the aesthetic that drives fashion e-commerce conversion in 2025.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Street Style Product Series</h3>
            <div style="background:rgba(31,41,55,0.3);padding:16px;border-radius:8px;margin:16px 0;">
                <p style="color:#fb7185;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Prompt template for candid fashion imagery:</p>
                <p style="color:#fb7185;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">"Candid street style photo of a [age/ethnicity] model wearing [garment description], walking on [location: cobblestone street / cafe terrace / urban rooftop]. Natural golden hour lighting. Shot on iPhone aesthetic — slightly warm tones, shallow depth of field. The model is mid-stride, looking away from camera. Background: blurred city life. No posing, no studio feel. The garment is the focus but the vibe is effortless."</p>
                <p style="color:#fb7185;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Variations per garment:</p>
                <p style="color:#fb7185;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">- Morning coffee shop scene (casual, warm)</p>
                <p style="color:#fb7185;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">- City crosswalk (urban, energetic)</p>
                <p style="color:#fb7185;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">- Park bench afternoon (relaxed, natural)</p>
                <p style="color:#fb7185;font-style:italic;">- Evening restaurant exterior (elevated, social)</p>
            </div>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Generate 4–6 lifestyle contexts per garment. Each context targets a different customer aspiration. The same blazer works for a power lunch scene, a gallery opening, a rooftop bar, and a creative studio — each attracting different buyer segments.</p>

Total: 115+ images from a unified visual theme. Production time: 2–3 days instead of the 6–8 weeks a traditional campaign shoot requires.</p>
