AI transforms onboarding into a cinematic welcome experience — engaging, professional, and consistent across every new hire, every office, every country.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Complete Onboarding Video Series</h3>

                <p style="color:#c084fc;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Onboarding video series structure:</p>
                <p style="color:#c084fc;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Module 1: "Welcome to [Company]" — 5-minute company story, mission, values (cinematic brand video style)</p>
                <p style="color:#c084fc;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Module 2: "Your First Week" — 8-minute guided walkthrough of first-week logistics, tools, and expectations</p>

        <section class="compliance-training">
            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 2: Compliance and Policy Training Videos</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Compliance training is mandatory but universally hated. Text-heavy slides, legal jargon, and zero engagement. AI transforms compliance content into scenario-based videos — showing real workplace situations where compliance matters, with branching decisions and consequences. Employees learn by watching situations play out, not by reading bullet points.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Scenario-Based Compliance Training</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Convert a 20-page compliance policy document into a series of 5-minute scenario videos. Each video presents a realistic workplace situation: a data breach incident, a harassment complaint, a conflict of interest scenario, a safety violation. Show the situation, the correct response, and the consequences of getting it wrong. Employees engage with the material emotionally, not just intellectually. Retention jumps from 15% to 60%+.</p>
        </section>

        <section class="skill-training">
            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 3: Scenario-Based Skill Training</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Soft skills, sales techniques, customer service, and leadership training all benefit from scenario-based video. Show the right approach and the wrong approach in realistic workplace scenarios. AI generates diverse scenarios covering different personalities, situations, and challenges — giving employees a library of situations to learn from.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Sales Training Scenario Library</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Generate a sales training video library: 20 scenario videos covering objection handling, needs discovery, closing techniques, and difficult conversations. Each scenario shows a realistic sales interaction — different industries, different buyer personalities, different challenges. Sales reps watch, learn, and discuss. The library becomes a reusable training resource updated quarterly with new scenarios.</p>
        </section>

        <section class="product-knowledge">
            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 4: Product Knowledge Video Libraries</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Companies with complex products need trained employees who understand features, benefits, and use cases. AI generates product training videos for every product in the catalog — feature walkthroughs, use-case demonstrations, competitive comparisons, and FAQ responses. Every employee, from sales to support, has access to consistent, up-to-date product knowledge.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Product Training Video System</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">For a company with 50 products, generate a training video library: 50 product overview videos (3 minutes each), 25 use-case demonstration videos (5 minutes each), and 10 competitive comparison videos (8 minutes each). Total: 85 videos covering the entire product portfolio. When products update, regenerate the affected videos. Training content stays current without re-shooting.</p>
