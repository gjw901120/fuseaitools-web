        <section class="logo-exploration">
            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 1: Logo Concept Exploration</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Logo design is the most iterative discipline in brand identity. A single logo might go through 50–200 concepts before the right direction emerges. Traditional design limits exploration to 10–20 concepts due to time and cost. AI enables unlimited logo exploration — trying every style, every metaphor, every typographic treatment until the perfect mark reveals itself.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Logo Exploration Sprint</h3>
            <div style="background:rgba(31,41,55,0.3);padding:16px;border-radius:8px;margin:16px 0;">
                <p style="color:#f97316;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Logo exploration prompt structure:</p>
                <p style="color:#f97316;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">"Logo design for [brand name]. Industry: [industry]. Brand personality: [modern/traditional/playful/serious/luxury/approachable]. Style explorations: minimalist wordmark, iconic symbol + text, monogram, lettermark, emblem, abstract mark. Color directions: [warm/cool/monochrome/vibrant]. Generate each style in 5 variations."</p>
                <p style="color:#f97316;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Exploration phases:</p>
                <p style="color:#f97316;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Phase 1: Broad exploration — 50+ concepts across all style categories</p>
                <p style="color:#f97316;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Phase 2: Direction shortlist — pick 3 directions, generate 20 variations each</p>
                <p style="color:#f97316;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Phase 3: Refinement — take winning direction, generate 30 detail variations</p>
                <p style="color:#f97316;font-style:italic;">Phase 4: Final presentation — 5 polished concepts for client review</p>
            </div>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">This sprint replaces the traditional 4–6 week logo exploration phase. The team enters refinement with a clear, tested direction — backed by visual evidence from hundreds of options, not just the first few ideas the designer sketched.</p>
        </section>

        <section class="identity-systems">
            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 2: Visual Identity System Generation</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">A brand identity is more than a logo. It's a complete visual system: color palette, typography hierarchy, pattern library, iconography style, photography direction, and layout principles. Generate comprehensive identity systems that extend the logo into a complete visual language.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Complete Identity System</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">From a finalized logo direction, generate the complete identity system: color palette (primary, secondary, accent, neutral — with hex codes), typography system (heading, subheading, body, caption fonts), brand patterns (geometric, organic, or abstract patterns derived from logo elements), iconography style (line weight, corner radius, fill style), photography direction (mood, lighting, composition rules), and layout grid system. All elements visually demonstrated in mock applications.</p>
        </section>

        <section class="brand-guidelines">
            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 3: Brand Guideline Document Creation</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Brand guidelines document how the identity system should be applied — logo usage rules, clear space requirements, minimum sizes, color applications, typography hierarchy, and do/don't examples. Generate comprehensive brand guideline documents with visual examples that make correct application obvious.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Brand Guidelines Document</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Create a 30-page brand guideline document: logo usage section (clear space, minimum size, color variations, incorrect usage examples), color system section (palette specifications, color combinations, accessibility requirements), typography section (font hierarchy, usage rules, pairing guidelines), pattern and texture section (brand patterns, background treatments), and application examples (business cards, letterhead, social media templates, merchandise). All generated with visual examples and clear specifications.</p>
        </section>

        <section class="brand-extensions">
            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 4: Brand Extension Design</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Brands extend across touchpoints: packaging, signage, vehicle wraps, uniforms, digital interfaces, social media, environmental design. Generate brand extension mockups showing how the identity system applies across every touchpoint — giving stakeholders confidence that the identity works everywhere it needs to.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Brand Extension Mockup Set</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Generate 30+ brand extension mockups: business cards (front/back), letterhead, envelope, presentation template, social media templates (5 platforms), packaging designs (3 product types), vehicle wrap, uniform design, signage concepts, website mockup, and environmental design (office/retail). All using the finalized identity system. These mockups sell the identity to stakeholders by showing it living in the real world.</p>
