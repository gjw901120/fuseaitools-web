        <section class="risk-assessment">
            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 1: AI-Assisted Risk Assessment & Credit Analysis</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Credit analysis remains one of the most labor-intensive processes in banking. A single commercial loan application requires financial statement analysis, industry risk assessment, management evaluation, collateral review, and covenant structuring — typically consuming 4–8 hours of analyst time. GPT can compress this to 30–60 minutes by automating the data gathering and preliminary analysis.</p>

            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Commercial Loan Credit Memo Generation</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Step 1: Input the borrower's financial data, business description, and loan request parameters.</p>
            <div style="background:rgba(31,41,55,0.3);padding:16px;border-radius:8px;margin:16px 0;">
                <p style="color:#10b981;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">"Analyze this commercial loan application and generate a credit memo:</p>
                <p style="color:#10b981;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Borrower: Manufacturing company, $15M annual revenue, 45 employees</p>
                <p style="color:#10b981;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Financials: 3 years of tax returns and P&L statements [attached]</p>
                <p style="color:#10b981;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Loan request: $2M equipment financing, 7-year term</p>
                <p style="color:#10b981;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">Generate:</p>
                <p style="color:#10b981;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">1) Financial spread analysis: key ratios (DSCR, leverage, liquidity) with 3-year trends</p>
                <p style="color:#10b981;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">2) Cash flow analysis: debt service coverage under base, stress, and downside scenarios</p>
                <p style="color:#10b981;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">3) Industry risk assessment: competitive position, market trends, key risks</p>
                <p style="color:#10b981;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">4) Management assessment: experience, succession planning, key-person dependency</p>
                <p style="color:#10b981;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">5) Collateral analysis: equipment valuation, secondary market liquidity</p>
                <p style="color:#10b981;font-style:italic;">6) Recommended structure: loan amount, terms, covenants, pricing rationale"</p>
            </div>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Step 2: GPT produces a complete credit memo draft with financial analysis, risk assessment, and recommended structure. The analyst reviews, adds judgment-based assessments (management quality, relationship value), and finalizes for credit committee.</p>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Key technique:</strong> Always include scenario analysis in credit memos. Ask GPT to model base, stress, and downside cases for debt service coverage. This shows the credit committee how the borrower performs under different economic conditions — not just whether they qualify today.</p>
