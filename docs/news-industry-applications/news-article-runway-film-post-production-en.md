            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: VFX Shot Pipeline</h3>

                <p style="color:#a855f7;font-style:italic;margin-bottom:8px;">1) Plate preparation: AI auto-masks subjects, separates foreground/background</p>

        <section class="compositing-roto">
            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 2: Compositing and Rotoscoping</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Rotoscoping — manually tracing subjects frame by frame to create masks — is the most tedious task in post-production. AI automates rotoscoping: subjects are segmented automatically with high accuracy, reducing hours of frame-by-frame work to minutes of setup and review.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: AI Rotoscoping Pipeline</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Process 100 VFX shots requiring rotoscoping. Traditional: 800+ hours of manual tracing. AI-assisted: generate initial masks automatically, review and refine where needed, output clean plates. Total time: 100–150 hours. The rotoscoping artist's role shifts from tracer to quality reviewer.</p>
        </section>

        <section class="color-grading">
            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 3: Color Grading and Look Development</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Color grading defines a film's visual identity. AI accelerates look development — generating color grade variations from reference images, matching grades across scenes, and applying consistent looks across the entire film. The colorist's creative decisions are amplified, not replaced.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Film Color Grade Pipeline</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Develop a film's color grade: create 5 look variations from reference imagery, select the final look with the director, apply the grade across all scenes with AI-assisted shot matching, and fine-tune individual shots. Total: 5 days instead of 3 weeks. The colorist spends time on creative refinement, not mechanical matching.</p>
        </section>

        <section class="cleanup-restoration">
            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 4: Cleanup and Restoration</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Every film needs cleanup — removing visible crew, equipment, boom mics, wires, and modern elements from period films. AI inpainting removes unwanted elements automatically, filling the gaps with contextually appropriate content. Film restoration uses AI to upscale, denoise, and repair damaged footage.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Film Cleanup and Restoration</h3>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Clean up 200 shots for a theatrical release: remove visible microphones, crew reflections, modern signage in period scenes, and wire work rigs. AI inpainting handles each removal in minutes. For archival footage restoration: upscale to 4K, remove grain and damage, stabilize shaky footage. A restoration project that took 6 months now takes 6 weeks.</p>
