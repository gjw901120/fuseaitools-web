AI accelerates this pipeline — processing documents in minutes instead of days, flagging issues for human review, and routing completed items to the right department.</p>
            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Permit Application Processing</h3>

The reviewer's job changes from data entry to decision-making.</p>
