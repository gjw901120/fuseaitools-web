            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 1: White-Background Product Photos</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">The baseline requirement: clean, consistent product shots on white or transparent backgrounds for marketplace listings.</p>

            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Product Catalog Generation</h3>
            <p style="color:#c084fc;font-style:italic;">"Generate a clean product photo of this ceramic mug on a pure white background. Studio lighting, soft shadows, centered composition, e-commerce catalog style. Show the mug at a 3/4 angle with the handle visible. High resolution, sharp focus."</p>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Use Flux's image-to-image capability: upload a reference photo or 3D render, then generate the catalog-ready version. Flux Kontext excels at maintaining product accuracy while controlling the visual environment.</p>

            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Key technique:</strong> Create a prompt template for your product category. Once you nail the lighting and angle formula, apply it consistently across your entire catalog for a cohesive look.</p>
        </section>

        <section class="lifestyle-scenes">
            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Workflow 2: Lifestyle Scene Creation</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Product photos sell specifications. Lifestyle images sell dreams. Flux generates contextual scenes that place your product in aspirational settings.</p>

            <h3 style="color:#f3f4f6;">Workshop: Context-Rich Lifestyle Shots</h3>
            <p style="color:#c084fc;font-style:italic;">"Place this ceramic mug in a cozy morning scene: wooden kitchen table, soft morning light through a window, a book open next to the mug, steam rising, warm color palette.             <p style="color:#d1d5db;">Generate multiple lifestyle contexts from a single product: kitchen counter, office desk, outdoor picnic, cozy reading nook. Each scene targets different customer segments and use cases.</p>
