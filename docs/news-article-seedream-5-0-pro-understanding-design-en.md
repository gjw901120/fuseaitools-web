        <section class="seedream-4">
            <h2>II. Seedream 4.0: From Text-to-Image to a Multimodal Creative Engine</h2>
            <p>Released in September 2025, Seedream 4.0 was the first watershed in ByteDance's image model line. Previous Seedream models were text-to-image; 4.0 was the first to pack <strong>text-to-image, image editing, multi-image reference, and storyboard/group generation</strong> into a single model.</p>
            <p>Its core capability can be summed up in one sentence: <strong>what words can't explain clearly, show with images.</strong></p>
            <p>Upload two portraits, then add a pose reference of two people "arms around each other", and tell the model "combine image 1 and image 2 into one frame, follow image 3's pose" — Seedream 4.0 directly generates a natural "group photo", with poses, expressions, and composition that look genuinely photographed.</p>
            <p>This multi-image fusion ability was, at the time, one of the few features able to match <strong><a href="/home/nano-banana/pro-generate">Nano Banana Pro</a></strong>. And Seedream 4.0 had one extra hand: <strong>multi-image output</strong> — from the same reference set, it generates 4-6 coherent comic panels or a poster series in one pass, with character appearance consistent across every frame.</p>
            <p>In evaluation, Seedream 4.0's combined Elo score across text-to-image and image editing had surpassed <a href="/home/nano-banana">Nano Banana</a> in the Seed team's test system. This established the core positioning of ByteDance's image model: benchmarking against <a href="/home/nano-banana">Nano Banana</a>, but with better Chinese, lower prices, and faster iteration.</p>
        </section>

        <section class="seedream-5">
            <h2>III. Seedream 5.0: From "Drawing Well" to "Thinking Right"</h2>
            <p>If 4.0's upgrade was "multimodal capability", the core upgrade of the 5.0 series is <strong>intelligence</strong>.</p>

            <h3>5.0 Lite: The First Time an Image Model "Thinks"</h3>
            <p>The 5.0 Lite preview, released in February 2026, introduced <strong>deep reasoning</strong> to Seedream for the first time. It doesn't just obediently draw — it draws only after "understanding".</p>
            <p>A typical test scenario: ask the model to reason through a Go/weigh-qi move — "after placing the white stone, capture the black stone" — and the model "thinks" about the placement rather than randomly generating a board image. In another scenario, facing a pile of scattered parts, the model infers the object type without the user pointing out attributes, and completes an "assembly".</p>
            <p>The Lite version also introduced <strong>real-time retrieval-augmented generation</strong> for the first time — it can pull the latest information online and quickly generate illustrations for trending events. This is effectively a "constantly updated knowledge base" attached to the model.</p>
            <p>Officially, ByteDance summarized the Lite positioning in one phrase: <strong>"smarter, more professional"</strong>. You can try both Lite modes directly on FuseAI Tools: <a href="/home/seedream/5-lite-text-to-image">5 Lite Text-to-Image</a> and <a href="/home/seedream/5-lite-image-to-image">5 Lite Image-to-Image</a>.</p>

            <h3>5.0 Pro: From "Thinking" to "Understanding Design"</h3>
            <p>In July 2026, 5.0 Pro was officially released. If the Lite version answered "can it think correctly", the Pro version answers "can it design well".</p>
            <p>Officially, the Pro upgrade is summarized as four capability breakthroughs:</p>

            <h4>1. Complex Information Visualization</h4>
            <p>Accurately converts high-density data, concepts, and text into professionally laid-out infographics. In tests, the model generated professional infographics on topics like "cost breakdown for embodied-intelligent robotics deployment", splitting hardware costs, software costs, and commercial adoption metrics into three columns, with icons, charts, and section headings. Chinese text rendering still shows errors, but structural design and information organization are approaching usable levels.</p>

            <h4>2. Interactive Precise Editing</h4>
            <p>This is the Pro version's most hardcore capability. Users can <strong>point, box-select, or scribble</strong> on an image, then tell the model "change this part to X" — the model recognizes the region's semantics and modifies only the specified area, preserving perspective, shadows, and ambient lighting.</p>
            <p>In tests, the model accurately recognized a red box-selected region and replaced a chair with dark-green velvet material while keeping the chair's position, perspective, and surrounding ambient light intact. It also supports <strong>layer separation</strong> — splitting a full poster into 10+ independent layers (text, subject, background, decorations), each retaining transparency and freely draggable.</p>

            <h4>3. Realistic Photography and Portrait Texture</h4>
            <p>In a sushi ad photography test, rice grains, fish roe, and salmon texture were richly detailed — already showing strong commercial photography quality. In a handwritten math homework photo test, it even generated details like "the imprint left on the front of the page from writing on the back".</p>

            <h4>4. Native Multilingual Input and Generation</h4>
            <p>Supports direct input and high-quality rendering across a dozen-plus common world languages, handling tasks like "translate an English menu into Chinese while keeping the original layout".</p>

            <h2>IV. Shortcomings and Controversies</h2>
            <p>Seedream 5.0 Pro is not flawless. Real-world testing exposed several clear weaknesses:</p>
            <ol>
                <li><strong>Complex Chinese infographics remain a weakness.</strong> In the "embodied-intelligent robotics cost breakdown" test, "伺服电机" (servo motor) appeared with a noticeable typo; text rendering errors also appeared in an "AI chip HBM explainer" infographic.</li>
                <li><strong>Fine UI design completion is insufficient.</strong> The overall completion of an e-commerce first-screen UI still trails real product website standards.</li>
                <li><strong>A gap with <a href="/home/gpt-image/v2-text-to-image">ChatGPT Images 2.0</a>'s "indistinguishable from reality".</strong> In cases like Sam Altman's social-media screenshot or a Peking University journal paper page, Seedream 5.0 Pro still clearly lags <a href="/home/gpt-image/v2-image-to-image">ChatGPT Images 2.0</a>.</li>
                <li><strong>Multi-character consistency issues.</strong> In group-image generation where two or more people appear in different scenes, occasional "face bleed" or "identity confusion" occurs.</li>
            </ol>
        </section>

        <section class="pricing">
            <h2>V. Pricing and Ecosystem</h2>
            <p>Seedream 5.0 Pro's API pricing strategy is aggressive: for output images up to 2.36 million pixels, <strong>¥0.3 per image</strong>; above that, <strong>¥0.6 per image</strong>. The first input image is free, with subsequent input images at ¥0.02 each.</p>
            <p>At 1K resolution for comparison: <a href="/home/nano-banana/nano-banana-2">Nano Banana 2</a> is about $0.067 (≈¥0.48), <a href="/home/gpt-image/v2-text-to-image">ChatGPT Images 2.0</a> is about ¥0.5-1, and Seedream 5.0 Pro's ¥0.3 is clearly competitive on price.</p>
            <p>Seedream 5.0 Pro is currently live in the Volcano Ark experience center and will roll out to Doubao and Jimeng in turn.</p>

        <section class="implications">
            <h2>VI. Implications for AI Tool Directories</h2>
            <h3>1. From "Text-to-Image Evaluation" to "Editing Capability Evaluation"</h3>
            <p>Seedream 5.0 Pro's strongest feature is not "generation quality" but <strong>interactive precise editing</strong>. Tool directories should expand evaluation dimensions from "who draws best" to "who edits accurately, who controls stably, who can split layers". Real-world scenarios like "box-select, replace material, and keep ambient light unchanged" reflect a model's true usability far better than comparing pure image quality.</p>

            <h3>2. Capture the Tutorial Dividend of "Interactive Editing"</h3>
            <p>Pixel-level editing, layer separation, and multi-image fusion look cool — but users don't know how to actually use them. What tool directories can produce is not "feature introductions" but hands-on tutorials: how do you use box-select to swap a product's color without breaking perspective? How do you use layer separation to create editable PSD assets? This kind of deep content is extremely scarce right now.</p>

            <h3>3. Chinese Infographics Are a "Test Field Worth Cultivating"</h3>
            <p>5.0 Pro's performance on complex Chinese infographics is not yet stable — which is exactly a window of opportunity. Tool directories can continuously track Seedream's iteration progress in infographic scenarios, publishing series content like an "annual infographic capability tracker" to build a professional cognition moat.</p>

            <h2>Conclusion: The Race Is Shifting from "Model Capability" to "Workflow Efficiency"</h2>
            <p>In less than a year, Seedream completed four iterations from 3.0 to 5.0 Pro. It didn't obsess over "pixel precision"; instead it chose a path closer to creators' real needs: from <strong>"drawing well"</strong> to <strong>"thinking right"</strong>, then to <strong>"modifying effectively"</strong>.</p>
            <p>It's still far from perfect — Chinese text rendering still errs, fine UI design isn't stable enough, and it still trails <a href="/home/gpt-image/v2-text-to-image">ChatGPT Images 2.0</a>. But its direction of evolution is clear: in the image generation race, ByteDance is using price, speed, Chinese comprehension, and iteration cadence to build its own "Chinese <a href="/home/nano-banana">Nano Banana</a>" ecosystem. Start from the <a href="/home/seedream">Seedream hub</a> on FuseAI Tools to experience where this path is heading.</p>
            <p>For tool directories, rather than chasing every new model release, the deeper thread is worth understanding: <strong>the competition in AI image generation is shifting from "model capability" to "workflow efficiency".</strong></p>
