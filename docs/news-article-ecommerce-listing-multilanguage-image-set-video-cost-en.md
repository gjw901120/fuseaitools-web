            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Pitfalls: Five Ways a Listing Catalog Month Overruns</h2>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Re-prompting localized infographics instead of editing them.</strong> Every fresh generation re-rolls the product, the layout, and the units a compliance reviewer already approved — at 24 credits each. Overlay translation is an edit at 6. This one reflex turns a 432-credit line into a 1,728-credit line.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Rendering dense infographics at 1K.</strong> The callouts and spec tables go unreadable at zoom, the listing converts worse, and you re-render at 2K anyway. On the Pro lane, 2K (24) is the correct floor for anything with text; the 1K saving is a trap you pay back double.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Asking for a transparent main image at 2K.</strong> The alpha flag is 1K-only; request it higher and the order can bill the 18-credit tier and still hand you an opaque file. The pure-white rule means you want the cutout at 1K and composite to white yourself — transparency sets the resolution.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Letting the main video run long.</strong> Listing video bills per second; at 27/s on the 720p lane, a 15-second cut costs almost twice an 8-second one and the marketplace caps useful length far below that anyway. Eight seconds, one idea, done.</p>
            <p style="color:#d1d5db;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Wordy voice-over scripts.</strong> Multilingual TTS bills UTF-8 bytes, so a script that drifts past a thousand bytes rounds up into a second 20-credit block for the same sentence. Keep each read to one tight line and the audio tail stays proportionate to the videos.</p>
