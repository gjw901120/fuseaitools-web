        <section class="pitfalls">
            <h2 style="color:#f9fafb;border-bottom-color:#374151;">Localization Failure Modes</h2>
            <ul style="color:#d1d5db;">
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Re-rendering is camouflage for "we didn't plan layers."</strong> Spot re-gens per market usually mean the master was built with baked-in text that can't be edited. Decompose at production time — Flash at 6 credits a frame makes pre-planning nearly free.</li>
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Translation bloat is a billing line.</strong> German and French narration routinely run 20–30% longer than English; that inflates dubbing (20/1k chars), forces re-timing, and can push the spot over platform length caps. Give the Station 1 prompt a character budget per language and enforce it.</li>
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Subtitling the mixed master instead of the dub.</strong> Captions burned from the English audio will drift from the Japanese performance — timestamps, breath, emphasis. Six credits per language to transcribe the actual dub is the whole fix.</li>
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Treating every market as marquee.</strong> Station 5 is one shot in one market at 96 credits. Do it in all four and the invoice more than doubles; let market revenue data, not enthusiasm, promote a second market to a hero re-render.</li>
                <li style="margin-bottom:6px;"><strong style="color:#f9fafb;">Capability is not clearance.</strong> Local landmark plates and voice profiles are generation capabilities, not rights to imply endorsement. Keep the CTA and trademark layers your own.</li>
            </ul>
